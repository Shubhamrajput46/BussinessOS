FAQ

Questions, answered

Is my business data separate from other companies?

Yes. Every workspace is isolated, and users only ever see data belonging to their own organisation.

Can my team use it on a phone?

Yes. The whole app is responsive, with a mobile menu and tables that reshape into easy-to-read cards.

What if I forget my password?

Use “Forgot password?” on the sign-in page. We email a secure, time-limited link to create a new one.

How does the AI forecast work?

It studies your daily sales history and projects a trend for the next 7, 14 or 30 days. It needs orders on at least two different days.

Can I connect my own app or website?

Yes. Every module is available through the REST API with JWT login and full documentation.
